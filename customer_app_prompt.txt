================================================================================
MASTER AI ENGINEERING PROMPT: TRANSFORM CLONED POS APP INTO "CITY COSMETICS DIRECT"
================================================================================

Role & Goal:
You are an expert Flutter & Supabase mobile app architect. This repository was cloned from CityPOS (the internal staff POS/Admin app). Your goal is to transform this codebase into a lightweight, secure, and beautiful B2B Customer Ordering Mobile Application ("City Cosmetics Direct") for trusted wholesale and retail clients with integrated Paystack payment support.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 0: ENVIRONMENT CONFIGURATION (.env Setup)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Reuse the same SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY from the original app so both apps communicate with the same Supabase database.
2. REMOVE sensitive admin keys (such as SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY).
3. Add PAYSTACK_PUBLIC_KEY=pk_live_xxxx... for client-side Paystack payment initialization.
4. Keep ARKESEL_API_KEY and ARKESEL_SENDER_ID for sending automated customer SMS notifications.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: CODEBASE CLEANUP & SECURITY STRIPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Delete or disable all internal staff and administrative screens to eliminate security risks and unnecessary bloat:
1. Delete internal admin screens in `lib/screens/admin/` (except data models and read-only services needed for catalog).
2. Delete POS Cashier checkout & Till management in `lib/screens/cashier/`.
3. Delete Warehouse intake, processing, and internal dispatch screens in `lib/screens/warehouse/`.
4. Delete internal staff salaries, expenses, GRA tax compliance, and system audit trail code.
5. Retain shared core models (`product.dart`, `customer_model.dart`, `customer_order_model.dart`), Supabase configuration (`supabase_config.dart`), theme, and image caching utilities.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: AUTHENTICATION & ONBOARDING FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Implement the customer authentication flow in `lib/screens/auth/`:

A. Customer Sign-In Screen (`customer_login_screen.dart`):
   - Input: Phone Number + Secret 4-Digit Passcode.
   - Authenticates against the `customers` table in Supabase.
   - If `status == 'pending_approval'`, redirect to `PendingApprovalScreen`.
   - If `status == 'suspended'`, display message: "Account suspended. Please contact City Cosmetics management."
   - If `status == 'active'`, proceed to `CustomerHomeScreen`.

B. Customer Sign-Up Screen (`customer_signup_screen.dart`):
   - Form Fields:
     1. Full Name (Contact Person)
     2. Phone Number (Unique Login ID)
     3. Business / Shop Name (e.g. "Grace Cosmetics Boutique")
     4. Business Location / Delivery Address
     5. Secret 4-Digit Passcode (with confirm passcode)
   - On Submit: Creates record in `customers` table with `status = 'pending_approval'`.
   - Navigation: Redirects to `PendingApprovalScreen`.

C. Pending Approval Screen (`pending_approval_screen.dart`):
   - Friendly UI informing the customer:
     "Thank you for registering with City Cosmetics! Your business account is currently under review by our team. You will receive an SMS notification once approved."
   - Includes a "Refresh Status" button and "Call Support" quick action.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: CORE CUSTOMER APP FEATURES & NAVIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Implement a clean bottom navigation bar with 3 main tabs:

TAB 1: Live Catalog & Product Search (`catalog_screen.dart`)
   - Product Grid: Displays active products with high-quality photos, names, categories, and brands.
   - Stock Availability: Clear badges ("In Stock", "Low Stock", "Boxes Available").
   - Tiered Pricing: Displays unit prices based on customer's assigned price tier (`retail`, `wholesale`, `vip`).
   - Packaging Selector: Option to choose quantity in Pieces (Pcs) or Boxes / Cartons.
   - Search & Category Filters: Filter by Skincare, Haircare, Fragrance, Makeup, etc.

TAB 2: Shopping Cart, Checkout & Paystack Integration (`cart_checkout_screen.dart`)
   - Review Cart Items: Update quantities or packaging units (Pcs/Boxes).
   - Delivery Preferences: Option for "Store Pickup (HQ)" or "Delivery to Business Address".
   - Payment Method Selection:
     1. Paystack (Mobile Money / Card): Integrates Paystack inline SDK / WebView for instant MoMo (MTN, Telecel) or Visa/Mastercard payment in GHS. Saves `paystack_reference` on success and marks `payment_status = 'paid'`.
     2. Add to Credit Balance: Available if customer's order total is within their assigned `credit_limit`. Sets `payment_status = 'credit'`.
     3. Pay on Delivery (COD): Cash or MoMo on arrival. Sets `payment_status = 'unpaid'`.
   - Submit Order: Inserts a new order into `customer_orders` table with status `'pending'` and displays an order confirmation receipt with Order #.

TAB 3: My Orders & Credit Statement (`my_orders_screen.dart`)
   - Live Order Progress: List of active and past orders with live status badges (`Pending` → `Approved` → `Dispatched` → `Delivered`) and payment badges (`Paid via Paystack`, `Credit`, `COD Unpaid`).
   - Order Details: Expand any order to view line items, quantities, and delivery notes.
   - Credit / Debt Balance Summary: Card showing current total debt balance, credit limit, and payment instructions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Theme Colors: Primary Maroon (`#6B1111`) / Accent Gold & Green.
